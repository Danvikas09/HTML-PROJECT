Do you feel like you can’t speak freely about your feelings?

When someone shuts down your voice, dismisses your opinions, or threatens you for speaking up, it’s emotional abuse. A healthy relationship allows open communication and respect.

What to do:

  • Speak to a friend, teacher, or counsellor about how you feel.
  • Don’t ignore your emotions — your feelings are valid.
  • If communication feels unsafe, plan a safe way to seek help.
