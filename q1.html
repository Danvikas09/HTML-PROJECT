Do you feel scared when your partner gets angry?

Feeling scared when your partner gets angry may be a sign of emotional or physical abuse. In a healthy relationship, disagreements happen — but fear should never be part of it.

What to do:

  • Talk to a trusted friend, relative, or counselor.
  • Reach out to local helplines for confidential advice.
  • If you ever feel unsafe, leave the area and seek help immediately.
