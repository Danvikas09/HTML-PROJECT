Have you been physically hurt, insulted, or isolated from others?

Physical harm, insults, or isolation are all strong indicators of abuse. No one has the right to hit, threaten, or separate you from loved ones.

What to do:

  • If you’re in immediate danger, contact 112 (Police Helpline) or go to a safe place.
  • Reach out to local NGOs or trusted people for protection and advice.
  • Remember, abuse is never your fault — and help is available.
