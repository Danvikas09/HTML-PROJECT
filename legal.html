Your Legal Rights

Victims of domestic violence are protected under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005. The law provides protection, residence rights, and maintenance support.

Steps You Can Take

  1. Contact a local Protection Officer or Police Station.
  2. File a complaint under the Domestic Violence Act.
  3. Approach a Legal Aid Cell for free legal help.
  4. Seek a Protection Order or Residence Order.

Where to Get Help

Contact the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) or your State Legal Services Authority (SLSA).
