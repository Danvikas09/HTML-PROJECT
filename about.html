Who We Are

The Domestic Violence Support Portal is an initiative created to raise awareness about domestic abuse, support victims, and provide safe access to legal and counselling resources. Our mission is to make sure no one suffers in silence — help should always be within reach.

Our Mission

We aim to break the cycle of violence by spreading awareness, providing education, and connecting victims with reliable support systems. We believe that change begins with compassion, awareness, and the courage to speak up.

Why We Created This Platform

  • To provide a safe online space for victims to find help without fear.
  • To guide users towards counselling, legal aid, and protection services.
  • To educate people on recognizing the signs of abuse early.
  • To encourage community support and social responsibility.

What We Offer

  1. Emergency Information: Quick access to national helplines and police support.
  2. Legal Guidance: Learn about your rights, laws, and how to file complaints safely.
  3. Counselling Support: Connect with trained professionals who can listen and guide you.
  4. Awareness Articles: Read stories and facts that empower people to act and help others.

Our Vision

We envision a society where every individual feels safe, valued, and respected — where violence and fear are replaced with empathy, equality, and justice. Through education and collective action, we can build a future free from abuse.

Join Us in Making a Difference

Every small step matters. Share this website with others, learn the signs of abuse, and become a voice for change. Together, we can make homes and communities safe for everyone.
