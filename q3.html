Does anyone control your money, phone, or social life?

When someone controls your access to money, phone, or friendships, it’s known as financial or controlling abuse. Abusers often try to isolate victims from support networks or independence.

What to do:

  • Recognize that control over your personal life is not love — it’s manipulation.
  • Try to keep personal documents, some savings, or trusted contacts safe.
  • Reach out to a counsellor or local women’s help group for confidential support.
