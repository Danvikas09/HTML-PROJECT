Welcome to the Domestic Violence Help Portal

Domestic violence affects people of all genders, ages, and backgrounds. It can be emotional, physical, sexual, or financial — and it is never acceptable. This platform offers counselling, legal help, and awareness resources for individuals seeking safety and support.

Our Goals

  • Provide quick and easy access to emergency helplines and legal resources.
  • Offer confidential counselling and safe communication channels.
  • Spread awareness and educate people about their rights.
  • Encourage victims and bystanders to report abuse safely.

Know the Signs – Quick Questions

If you or someone you know might be in danger, click on a question to learn more:

  • Do you feel scared when your partner gets angry?
  • Are you often blamed for things that are not your fault?
  • Does anyone control your money, phone, or social life?
  • Have you been physically hurt, insulted, or isolated from others?
  • Do you feel like you can’t speak freely about your feelings?

How You Can Help Others

  1. Listen without judging anyone who opens up about abuse.
  2. Share verified helpline numbers or safe contacts.
  3. Encourage victims to seek counselling or legal support.
  4. Speak out — silence protects the abuser, not the victim.
